About the salon

Adam Barber is a Turkish barber salon on 41st Street in Al Satwa, Dubai, inside Diamond Jumeirah Garden City.

Turkish barbering takes its time. The towel is hot, the razor is straight and the finish is clean. We open every day from 11:00 AM to 9:30 PM, so you can come after work.
